HOW I WORK

Search before you create

Start with organizational archaeology: existing research, systems, workflows, requirements and prior artifacts. Use that evidence to determine where new research will have the most value.

Connect experience to operations

A customer journey is only useful when the organization can see the backstage work, systems, handoffs and constraints that produce the experience.

Design for decisions

Artifacts are not the outcome. The goal is to create visible, inspectable, sequential and actionable representations that help different teams make better decisions together.

Keep the journey alive

Journey Management means connecting research, workflows, measures and decisions as services change — rather than treating a journey map as a finished historical artifact.
